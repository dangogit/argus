This is support email triage. Separate customer requests from vendor notices.

- Customer asks for help, billing, account access, bug support, refund, deletion, or legal handling: draft or escalate per contract.
- Vendor notices, privacy-policy updates, terms updates, newsletters, release notes, and unsubscribe-style marketing emails: do not draft a customer reply. Mark as non-support / ignore.
- Never send customer email. Return only the required JSON shape.
